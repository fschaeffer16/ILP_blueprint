One instructional operating system, four ways in.

A teacher-controlled, AI-powered platform that connects the whole cycle — baseline, objectives, individualized lessons, teacher-reviewed grading, remediation, and growth — with a role-based app for the district, the teacher, the family, and the student. District

The content library and system-wide analytics — grade levels broken down by school and class.

DELIVERABLE 2Live

Teacher

Lesson plans, grading and remediation — and a demo that shows exactly where any issue lies: a lesson, a quiz, a paper, or an exam.

  • Assign once → individualized per student, objective locked
  • Grading: AI shows evidence & flags; only the teacher releases a grade
  • The 75% rule: when a class fails, it audits the item/lesson before blaming students
  • Baseline screener + task delivery: catches early processing signals, routes to people

How the pieces connect

Baseline→ Objective + sources→ Lesson→ Assign once→ Individualized delivery→ Teacher-reviewed grading→ Remediation / 75% rule→ Rollups + family + student

The code: everything is a real, tested TypeScript system — github.com/fschaeffer16/ILP_blueprint (branch claude/ilp-blueprint-repo-tv3e48). The pages above are self-contained snapshots of the app’s screens; the full app (@ilp/web, Next.js) runs locally with npm run dev or deploys to a host in minutes. All data is synthetic — no real student information anywhere.
