Study guides

Fractions as equal parts

Explain and represent a fraction as equal parts of a whole.
Key ideas
numerator · denominator · equal parts · whole/part relationship
Worked example
A rectangle is split into 3 equal parts and 1 part is shaded. There are 3 equal parts, so the denominator is 3. One part is shaded, so the numerator is 1. The shaded amount is 1/3.
Watch out for
“larger denominator means larger fraction”

Comparing fractions

Compare two fractions with the same numerator or the same denominator, and justify the comparison.
Key ideas
numerator · denominator · same whole
Watch out for
“a bigger bottom number always means a bigger fraction”
